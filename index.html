Day 12  ·  Thursday
Capri Day Trip
Isle of Capri · Blue Grotto · Gardens of Augustus
🇮🇹 Italy
🚂
Transport
Luggage stays at Rome hotel — daypack only. Train Roma Termini → Napoli Centrale: 7–7:30am Frecciarossa 1h10. Ferry Naples Molo Beverello → Capri: 50 min, €20–25/person (SNAV or Caremar). Funicular up to Capri Town: €2. Ferry back ~5pm. Train Naples → Rome: arrives 6:30–7pm.
🌊
Morning
Very early departure — 7am train from Rome to Naples, ferry to Capri. Arrive ~10–11am. Funicular up to Capri Town.
🌺
Afternoon
Gardens of Augustus (panoramic views of Faraglioni rocks), Villa Jovis viewpoint, boat tour around the island (€15–20/person). Catch ferry back ~5pm.
🍽
Dinner
Pick up luggage at Rome hotel. Last dinner — BaGhetto or Yotvata in the Jewish Ghetto.
⚠️
Warning
⚠️ ZERO kosher food on Capri — pack ALL food from Rome the night before. Full day from Rome supplies needed.
Rome Hotel
Night 3 of 3. Leave luggage here during Capri day trip.
⚠️ Absolutely no kosher food on Capri. Pack protein bars, sealed kosher meals, fruit, and snacks from Rome the night before. This is non-negotiable.
